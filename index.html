About Us

826 is an Auto Detailing & Paint Protection Film (PPF) Shop was established in November 2024. The business was founded after the owner decided to part ways with a detailing shop partnership that lasted for six years. With extensive hands-on experience and passion for automotive care, the owner built 826 to offer not only professional detailing services but also high-quality Paint Protection Film installation.

The owner is also recognized as one of the top Sales Managers in Toyota, bringing strong automotive knowledge, customer service excellence, and industry expertise into the business. This background ensures that every vehicle handled at 826 meets high standards of quality, protection, and presentation. At 826, we are committed to enhancing, and protecting every vehicle through skilled craftsmanship, premium products, and meticulous attention to detail. Our goal is to provide long-lasting protection while maintaining showroom-quality results for every client.

Our installer team consists of skilled professionals who previously worked in Qatar, bringing with them more than 8 years of experience in automotive protection and detailing services, ensuring precision, reliability, and world-class workmanship.
